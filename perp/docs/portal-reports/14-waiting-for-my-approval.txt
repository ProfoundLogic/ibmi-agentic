REQUISITION_LINE: the items requested.

"Over auto limit".
"Low confidence".
"Not scored".
Colour how long each one has been waiting: 0-1 days fine, 2-3 amber, 4 or more red.

What I'd like to see:
Tiles: waiting, past need-by, value waiting, and eligible for auto.
The queue, with a confidence gauge and an action.
A panel of the requisitions CoderFlow could approve.
The current policy values.
A small list of draft requisitions that may arrive soon.

Make a friendly empty state ("Your approval queue is clear", plus the date of the last decision), because the queue may be empty.
