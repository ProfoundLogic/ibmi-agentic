Data in library PERPDEMO:
REQUISITION_HEADER, REQUISITION_LINE, PO_HEADER, PO_LINE, PO_LINE_SCHEDULE, ITEM, ITEM_LOT, ITEM_VENDOR, ITEM_VENDOR_PRICE, VENDOR, PERP_USER and COMPANY_CONFIG.

Requisitions waiting for approval (status SUBMITTED).
Approved requisitions with no PO, by count and value.
Items to reorder (available plus open PO quantity at or below the reorder point; ignore ITEM.qty_on_order).
Items at critical level.
Late deliveries (due date from the schedule, else expected date, else order date plus lead time), by count and value.
Open PO value.
Draft POs.
Items heading over max.
Lot mismatches (on-hand not equal to the lot total), by count and value.
On-order mismatches.

CoderFlow approvals outside the COMPANY_CONFIG policy.

The KPI tiles in four rows: Buy, Receive, Stock integrity, and Approvals. Each tile is coloured by severity and links to its detail report.
A "do these first" list of the top 5 actions by dollar impact, each with an owner.
A small table of open exceptions per person.
