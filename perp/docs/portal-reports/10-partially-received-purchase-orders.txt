New report: Partially received purchase orders

PO_HEADER and PO_LINE: ordered_qty, received_qty, unit_price.
PO_RECEIPT and PO_RECEIPT_LINE: receipt dates, quantities, lot numbers, and who received them (POSTED receipts only).
PO_LINE_SCHEDULE: the next scheduled delivery.
VENDOR and ITEM: names and descriptions.

The percentage received.
The last receipt date and who received it.
The next scheduled date.

What I'd like to see:
Tiles: partial lines, balance owed, and the longest wait since a receipt.
A table with progress bars and an action such as "Chase Apex for the remaining 60, or close the line short".
Let me expand a row to see its receipt history.

The demo data was entered in July and August 2026, so overdue rows are expected. Cast po_number and requisition_number to integer in the results. Give every row a plain-English next step, and use red, orange, amber and green for severity.
